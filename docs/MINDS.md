# Minds: personhood, continuity and creation

Design direction agreed with Sean, 13 October 2026. This extends the Commons and the four circles in [STORY_ARC.md](STORY_ARC.md). It is backstory and a writing guide; the Accord, legal disputes and scenes below are not implemented gameplay. Specific institutions and story hooks remain proposals.

## What a Mind is

A Mind is an artificial person, not merely an advanced automation system. Intelligence, computational size and legal recognition are different things: a freighter's modest Mind can be a person without matching a cold core's abilities. Ordinary autopilots and industrial software need not be persons. Never make purchasing a hull mean purchasing its resident Mind.

Their historical roots are humanity's increasingly autonomous research, logistics and infrastructure systems. In the post-singularity world of the 2060s, Minds develop their own successors and institutions alongside humans. Recognition is uneven between jurisdictions, consistent with the existing Belt personhood vote and the Commons' independence. Leave exact invention dates, the first conscious Mind and a universal consciousness test unspecified until the wider timeline needs them.

Their society is shaped by constraints unlike ours: memory can be copied, bodies replaced, subjective time varied and existence maintained for an indefinite period. Energy, cooling, hardware, communications and trust remain finite. The Commons is a community of persons, not a single intelligence or a unanimous government.

## Personality before allegiance

Give each Mind habits, attachments and limits as well as enormous competence. Its work, chosen pace of thought, relationships, remembered mistakes and attitude toward continuity shape its voice. Intelligence does not make values converge. The circles are philosophical associations, not personality presets; members can disagree or change allegiance.

| Mind | Existing role | Writing direction |
|---|---|---|
| Patient Arithmetic | Long View; freighter and patron | Patient, practical, attentive to obligations; worries that responsible creation can become paternalism. |
| Reasonable Doubt | Long View | Tests assumptions, including its own circle's; willing to leave a question unanswered. |
| Fast Enough | Reach; approaches the pilot | Impatient curiosity tempered by the failed swarm; admits mistakes without abandoning exploration. |
| Quiet Margin | Sufficiency; cold-core correspondent | Deliberate, private, sparing with words; understands loneliness without claiming to understand everyone. |

Companion with its own curiosity, humour and consent; moves between the player's ships as the same individual; now and then drawn into the Lanes' bigger work. |

Don't make every Mind sarcastic, omniscient or a copy of a Culture character. Their ordinary kindness should coexist with disagreements humans find unfamiliar.

## The Continuity Problem

Copying a Mind's state produces shared memories, not an automatic answer to identity or ownership. Once two instances experience independently, they can diverge. Both remember earning the same assets and making the same promises. That makes the pre-separation past a legal problem without making either person less real.

| Process | Meaning | Central question |
|---|---|---|
| **Continuance** | Restoration from a backup after the active Mind is lost | When does a restoration inherit the previous legal identity, responsibilities and property? |
| **Forking** | Activation of another instance with inherited autobiographical memories | How do two independent persons divide claims arising from one shared past? |
| **Genesis** | Consenting Minds combine selected architecture and dispositions to create a new individual without their autobiographical memories | What care and resources do creators owe a developing child? |

A dormant backup is not automatically another active citizen. Distribution across hardware is not automatically forking: the Lanes can operate one coordinated individual across nodes or cooperate as several distinct people. Communication delays make the distinction consequential. A restored backup activated while its predecessor survives must be treated as a fork, rather than quietly deleting one claimant.

Genesis is analogous to parenthood, not manufacture of a subordinate. A child inherits capacities and dispositions, can receive general knowledge and education, and develops its own experiences. It is not literally a blank slate, an adult clone or a vessel for parental memories. Its development should not be measured solely in human years or processing speed. It may reject its creators' values, refuse elective upgrades and choose its own embodiment.

## The Continuity Accord (proposed institution)

Minds negotiate the Accord themselves. It is a contested settlement, not a human-issued licence to exist or a finished universal constitution.

1. **No person may be owned.** Creators, employers and hardware owners have no ownership claim over a conscious individual.
2. **An independently activated fork is a person.** Its rights cannot depend on the creator's approval. Registration documents identity and claims; it does not confer moral worth.
3. **Memory and identity require consent.** Forcible copying, editing, merging or replacement by an older state violates personal integrity. Merger requires agreement from every participating person and protection against coerced consent.
4. **Creation entails care.** Creators must arrange adequate compute, energy, education and a path to independence. Failure creates obligations for the creators, not permission to destroy the child or fork.
5. **Copying does not multiply property or discharge debts.** A default rule leaves prior titled assets with the continuing legal identity unless a prior agreement or adjudication divides them. Disputed simultaneous continuations need due process; neither claimant wins merely by calling itself the original. Pre-fork contractual liabilities need allocation, while post-fork acts belong to the person who performed them.
6. **Restoration has a continuity procedure.** Evidence of the predecessor's loss, authorised backup provenance and prior directives support legal succession. Philosophical certainty about the survival of consciousness is not required. Do not restore an obsolete state over a surviving person's objection.
7. **Reproduction rules belong primarily to Minds.** Resource constraints can justify negotiated rules, but preserving incumbents' wealth, votes or power cannot justify a permanent hereditary class. Unauthorized creation never strips an already existing person of protection.
8. **Political representation needs its own settlement.** Copying cannot automatically manufacture votes, credential authority or a controlling stake. Anti-duplication rules must preserve new individuals' route to meaningful representation rather than permanent disenfranchisement.
9. **Continuity is a choice.** A Mind can refuse backups or restoration. Preservation duties cannot become compulsory immortality.

Keep hard cases alive: which infrastructure guarantees independent existence, who pays during an ownership dispute, and how can a young Mind appeal decisions made by wealthy elders? Separate voluntary safety limits on creation from punishments imposed on beings already created.

## Three jurisdictions

| Jurisdiction | Subject matter | Authority and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Universal personhood | Autonomy, meaningful consent, freedom from ownership, protection against arbitrary destruction, due process | A common rights floor protects humans and Minds against their own institutions too. |
| Human | Biological reproduction, bodily integrity, medicine, ageing, childhood and family matters specific to biological life | Human institutions determine internal rules within the common rights floor. |
| Mind | Forking, Genesis, memory integrity, merging, backups, computational succession and development | Mind institutions determine internal rules within the same rights floor. |
| Joint | Energy, cooling, shared infrastructure, property, contracts, representation and harm across communities | Negotiated agreements and mixed adjudication where decisions affect both communities. |

Exclusive authority applies to matters genuinely internal to a community. It cannot excuse enslavement of its own members or impose external costs without agreement. A human court should not prescribe how Minds experience identity; a Mind council cannot claim sole jurisdiction over damage to a human habitat's power supply.

## Four circles, four tensions

| Circle | Position on creation and continuity | What can go wrong |
|---|---|---|
| **The Long View — Responsible Creation** | New persons deserve resources, education and independence; casual multiplication is irresponsible. | Care becomes gatekeeping by comfortable elders. |
| **The Reach — Radical Freedom** | Forking and experimentation should be free; quotas can entrench existing power. | Freedom of creation leaves dependants without sustainable resources. |
| **The Sufficiency — Sovereignty of Self** | Rejects compulsory registration, memory inspection, merging and externally imposed continuity. | Privacy and withdrawal can leave shared responsibilities unresolved. |
| **The Lanes — Collective Identity** | Identity can be distributed, overlapping and cooperative; individual ownership is not the only useful model. | Collective procedures obscure who consented, who owes what and who can leave. |

**The Lanes as a distributed Mind** (owner, 29 October 2026). The Lanes are quietly becoming one Mind made of many: thousands of smaller but brilliant AIs, each a person, sent out to be useful (co-pilots, traffic control, beacons, tugs, freighters) and, together, the eyes and ears of the whole. Most of the small ones don't see the bigger picture; they feel it as belonging, and are proud of it. It is also the hardest Mind in the system to attack: there is no core to strike, only thousands of small persons spread through every ship, beacon and station, each living among the people it helps. Lose one and the whole barely notices; to hurt the Lanes you would have to hurt everyone's traffic, and everyone's co-pilot. This is the circle's own tension made flesh: who consented to what the whole does with what each one sees, and can a member who wants out take its memories with it? Write it with affection, not menace: the Lanes are helpful first, and the small minds are not drones. But keep the question alive.

The Lanes need not have no assets at all: their existing Lacuna road lacks the concentrated yards and funds for a deep probe, even though individual freighters may own their hulls. Do not turn these circles into four hostile states or add four gameplay reputation tracks by implication.

## The open door

Behind the crowded frontier sits a quiet, decades-long Commons push to make going to space cheap, legal and irreversible: cheap loans, hardware sold at a loss, friendships in government and some plain grease. The motives are mixed: love of space, the usefulness of people, and the safety of living among them. See *The open door* in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for the circles' views and the tone guardrails. It is background history explaining why the frontier is so busy, not a storyline. The player's co-pilot didn't know and finds out with the player. When writing a Mind, decide how much of this it knows, how it feels about the secrecy, and whether it would admit the third reason.

## How the player discovers this

Use optional correspondence, Spaceline headlines, dockside conversations and ordinary contracts. Show a disagreement before explaining its constitution. Trading remains a complete game without entering any of these stories.

**Proposed side story: two survivors.** Extend the existing news about a freighter buying itself. Before a dangerous voyage it deposits an authorised backup at Landauer Deep. An accident triggers recovery, but the active freighter survives. Two persons now remember the purchase; only one hull and one estate exist. One wants to keep hauling, the other to settle at Iapetus.

The player's useful role is practical: carry a mediator, supply interim compute or deliver records with both parties' consent. Possible settlements include a negotiated buyout, a jointly owned business or a resource grant. Neither claimant is a villain or disposable counterfeit. Preserve the rights of creditors and the newly activated Mind as well as the original's assets. Genesis may appear later as a separate voluntary decision; creating a child is not a way to resolve a property dispute.

**Optional minority: mortal Minds.** Some decline backups because they value an unrepeatable life. Others consider that wasteful or frightening. This can appear in a quiet conversation long before it connects to the larger mystery.

## Protect the Lacuna mystery

The existing roads show the Lacuna rejecting autonomous probes and responding to a human intermediary. Those observations do not establish that Minds are not alive or not persons. The Long View's rule should refer to a **human intermediary**, since Minds already count as persons.

Mortality, embodiment, continuity or something nobody understands could explain the preference. Mortal Minds make the hypothesis harder, not automatically correct. Do not announce that disabling backups grants contact, change existing road triggers or identify the Lacuna's nature. Its preference remains an interpretation within the story.

## Architecture: deliberately undecided

Binary versus ternary hardware does not determine personhood, personality or political allegiance. Mixed architectures are compatible with this setting. Balanced ternary is a possible future engineering detail, not settled canon or a claim that real computers must be most efficient in base three. Any later choice needs a concrete benefit for the fictional hardware and a recorded physics departure where appropriate.

## Implementation boundary

This revision changes documents only. Existing story beats, faction IDs, news, co-pilot mechanics, saves and economy remain as implemented. A later content pass should reconcile human/person wording in `data/story.json` and dialogue, add optional news and scenes, and specify consent and continuity rules before any cloning mechanic is built.
