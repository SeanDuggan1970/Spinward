## Description

---

**It is 2061, and the solar system is open for business.**

Spinward is a space trading game set in our own solar system. Every planet, moon and station moves on an orbit fitted to NASA JPL data, and every voyage is flown under real gravity. You start with a thirty-year-old Mule-class hauler, ten thousand credits and a berth at Kibo Ring in low Earth orbit. Everything else is up to you.

It's inspired by Elite (1984), but it isn't a remake. There are no pirates and no wars. The future here is hard work, hopeful, and getting richer every month, and full of potential for anyone brave or crazy enough to go and look.

### Trade your way out

- **Buy low, haul, sell high:** fourteen goods, from ice and regolith to helium-3 and medicine, across seventeen ports and three towns at the foot of space elevators. There are supply and demand you can feel, and production chains from the Moon's ice mines to the belt's smelters.
- **Fly every approach yourself:** dock by hand at spinning stations, Elite-style, with the classic 3D scanner. Or pay the tug.
- **Refit as you grow:** fit long-haul tanks, a 300-day hab, passenger berths, a lander and a mining rig, then a fusion drive that reaches Jupiter and Saturn.
- **Get known for being reliable:** courier jobs come to pilots with a name for it. Packages against the clock, passengers, pickups, and long hauls between worlds.
- **Buy tips at the Tip Line:** brokers sell what they hear about other ports, and some of them are even right.

### Fly the real solar system

- **The whole system:** Earth and Moon, Mars and its moons, the belt, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto out to Sedna, all on real orbits, at true scale.
- **Real voyages:** trips climb out of each gravity well, cross the Sun's domain on a guided burn-and-coast arc, and spiral down at the far end. Mars is a couple of months away in a starter ship, and Saturn takes a fusion refit and a season. Time compression runs up to a million times.
- **The ship view:** while you travel, your ship flies in the real sky. Drag to orbit the camera and scroll to zoom, or let the director frame the shots: a long lens with Saturn looming behind you, a dolly along the hull, the burn over Earth's limb.
- **Real shadows:** Saturn's rings follow Cassini's measurements, with the planet's shadow across them. Station approaches on Earth's night side are dark, lit only by the cities below.
- **Real danger:** you can hit stations, ships and drifting rocks. Hits damage whatever they land on, drives, tanks or cargo pods, and a hard enough one breaks the keel. The lifeboat and the insurance pool will get you home.

### Build the future together

- **Megaprojects:** everyone is building something, and every build needs haulers. Island One, a Bernal sphere at L5. The Concord Pair, two O'Neill cylinders over Ceres. The Tsiolkovsky Wheel, a Stanford torus. The Selene Ring, round the Moon. Solar-sail freighters, and space elevators on the Moon, Ceres and Mars. Each one pitches for backers, and backers earn rewards.
- **The Spaceline:** news from across the system, in Earth Standard Time. New projects are announced as they look for backers. New seams, new ships and new laws change the markets for good.
- **Ride the elevators:** take the Luna Line, the Piazzi Stalk or the Pavonis Line down to the towns at the bottom, with your cargo. Your weight falls to nothing on the way up.
- **Minds of their own:** in 2061 some AIs are asking to be counted as people, and some would like a home of their own. Landauer Deep, out at Saturn's moon Iapetus, is cold, quiet and built by minds. Visitors are welcome.
- **Something at a thousand AU:** the old probe Hermes-7 saw something out there. A few very patient minds would like a person to go and say hello.

### Controls

Keyboard, and the mouse at ports and in the ship view. Press **F1** at any time for every key.

- **Flying an approach:** W/S thrust, A/D and R/F strafe, the arrows pitch and yaw, Q/E roll, Shift boost, X brake.
- **In transit:** M changes view. Drag to orbit the ship camera, and scroll to zoom.
- **Time:** [ and ] slow down and speed up time, and P pauses.
- **Saves:** F5 quick-saves and F9 quick-loads.

### Playing in the browser

- **Browser:** a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox), with a window of 1280 × 800 or larger.
- **First load:** it downloads about 40 MB.
- **Click to play:** click the game once so it has keyboard focus.
- **Saves:** they're kept in your browser only.
- **Plotting a route:** it can pause for a second or two in the browser. That's normal.

### Status

Spinward is early and in active development. It's playable from your first trade to the edge of the system, and there's more to come: your own base in a hollowed asteroid, remappable controls and gamepads, and more of the system to build. Feedback is very welcome in the comments.
